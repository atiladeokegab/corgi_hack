# Her judgement is made once, and leaks seven times

A map of every problem Sofia faces that we can evidence, arranged as the life of a single
recommendation — from the moment she forms an opinion to the moment someone acts on it.

Sources: the 17-sheet case file (`evidence/`), the organiser/judge conversation transcript,
and Edmund's root-cause work in `README.md`. Sheet refs are given so every claim is checkable.

```
1. SHE FORMS IT  →  2. SHE RECORDS IT  →  3. SHE PUBLISHES IT  →  4. THEY ASK
                                                                        ↓
   7. SOMEONE BUYS  ←  6. THEY DECIDE  ←────────────  5. SHE ANSWERS BY HAND
```

Follow one recommendation from her head to someone's wardrobe. It loses something at every
handoff. Numbers below are stable IDs — cite them rather than restating the problem.

## The rule over all of it

> *"Sofia operates in chaos. This is part of her process and should be respected."* — Edmund's RCA,
> corroborated by the judges transcript (notebooks, Notion, notes apps, voice notes, pads, sticky notes)

**A product that asks her to tidy up is dead**, and a real creator on the judging panel will say so
in about four seconds. Whatever gets built has to eat the chaos as it is.

---

## Where this points

Three stages leak hardest:

- **Stage 3** — her best judgement never ships
- **Stage 5** — spending it by hand costs her everything
- **Stage 6** — they decide alone, over days, and leave

**Plug 3 and 5 and you get 6 for free:** capture what she has already decided, and let it answer
without her.

Two hard constraints on any answer: it must beat the four named ManyChat failures (32–34), and it
must produce *varied* answers, because #35 rules out templates.

## Assumptions we are making

The organisers permit assumptions but require them to be stated. Running list:

1. Sofia's source notes are unstructured and multi-format; we choose a plausible representation.
2. Her revenue is affiliate-led, with gifted product and paid UGC alongside — the exact mix is
   unconfirmed and no claim should depend on it.
3. `evidence/wardrobe.csv` is 8 of 36 items; there is no numeric "Sofia rating" in the evidence,
   only her free-text verdict.
4. `intent.csv`'s `link_click` column is deliberately incomplete — that is the puzzle, not a defect.
