STEAL THE TASTE
Your mission is not to build a better link page. It is to make
Sofia’s judgement useful at scale.
THE OBJECTIVE
ONE PRODUCT. ONE AUDIENCE MEMBER. ONE DECISION MADE
BETTER.
Pick a person from the evidence, pick the decision they are stuck on,
and ship the thing that answers it the way Sofia would. If you cannot
name the person and the decision, you do not have a product yet.
YOUR BUILD MUST
E-08.1 Help a real audience member make a better decision.
E-08.2 Preserve a meaningful piece of Sofia’s judgement / taste.
E-08.3 Work with the evidence provided.
E-08.4 Be demonstrable in a few clicks.
E-08.5 Not require expensive AI inference to be impressive.
THINGS YOU DO NOT NEED
A giant model.
A huge backend.
A pitch deck pretending it is
a product.
STANDING ORDER
You have 8 hours. Ship the
sharpest proof of your idea,
not the largest codebase.
